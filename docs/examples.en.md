# OBS Remote: common tasks

[Home](./README.en.md) · [Installation](./INSTALLATION.en.md) · [Cloud access](./CLOUD.en.md)

The remote's interface is currently in Russian; the labels below include the Russian text shown on screen.

## Switch scenes from a phone

Open the remote on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi network and tap a scene. The list and active scene come directly from OBS.

## Prepare an image before showing it

1. Open **Sources** («Источники») → **Add** («Добавить») → **Image** («Картинка»).
2. The new layer appears to the right of the frame, and the editing workspace opens automatically.
3. Drag the layer into the frame. Use the corner handles to resize it, or enter its width, height, and rotation for precise control.
4. Select **Save to shelf** («На полку») if you want to use the same source in another scene.

## OBS audio

The **Mixer** («Микшер») lists sources for which OBS exposes audio controls. These can include microphones, browser sources, donation widgets, YouTube, Yandex Music, local files, and other compatible sources. Use the switches to mute or unmute them.

## Work from one screen

In **Studio** («Студия»), the frame and scenes are on the left, with the mixer and layers on the right. On a phone, panels are stacked vertically; the scene, layer, and audio buttons scroll to the relevant panel.

Scenes, sources, and settings remain in the user's OBS. The remote does not require a particular scene collection or service.
